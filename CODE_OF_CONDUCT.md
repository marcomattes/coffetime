# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Coffee Time is a small open-source project. We want participation — filing
issues, submitting pull requests, reviewing code, discussing design — to be
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level,
background, identity, or the amount of coffee they've had.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in comments, reviews, and discussion.
- Assume good faith; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept disagreement gracefully, and give and receive feedback in a
  professional, focused-on-the-work way.
- Respect other participants' time and privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Discriminatory jokes, language, or imagery.
- Threats of violence or intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information (their real name,
  address, or other identifying data) without their explicit consent —
  particularly relevant given this project's focus on privacy.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository's
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official Coffee Time
communication channel — and also applies when an individual is representing
the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project
maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to reach them privately
if no other contact is available). Reports will be reviewed and
investigated as promptly as circumstances allow, and handled with
discretion regarding the reporter's identity.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
fairly. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
